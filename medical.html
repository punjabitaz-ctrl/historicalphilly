When you need to be nearby

More like home, less like a hotel

Treatment and recovery can mean days or weeks away from home. A whole house gives you space, a kitchen and quiet, close to the hospitals people travel to Philadelphia for.

Room for the whole family

Three bedrooms, sleeping 8 to 10, so a patient, a partner, parents and siblings can stay together.

A real kitchen

Cook the food you can eat and keep it in a full-size fridge. Every house is an entire home with its own kitchen.

Laundry and parking

Rodman and Hancock have a washer and dryer in the home, and Hancock includes free parking, which matters on repeat trips.

Quiet streets

Residential blocks, not a hotel corridor. A calm place to rest between appointments.

Stairs & accessibility

Please note: Every one of our houses is a multi-level historic home. Bedrooms and bathrooms are reached by steep, narrow stairs, and there is no elevator or step-free route. If anyone in your party has limited mobility or small children, ask us before you book.

Which house

Choosing where

For the shortest trips to Pennsylvania Hospital, Wills Eye and Jefferson, Rodman House in Society Hill is closest on foot.

Hancock House and Catherine House in Queen Village are a few minutes farther, with free parking at Hancock. The two stand side by side, so a larger family can take both.

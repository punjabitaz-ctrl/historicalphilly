Stairs & accessibility

Please note: Hancock House is a multi-level historic home. Bedrooms and bathrooms are reached by steep, narrow stairs, and there is no elevator or step-free route. If anyone in your party has limited mobility or small children, ask us before you book.

The house

About Hancock

Hancock House is an entire historic rowhome on a quiet cobblestone street in Queen Village, near Center City and steps from some of the city's best-known restaurants.

It sleeps up to ten, with a finished basement that doubles as a workspace, a gas kitchen with a dishwasher, and free parking, which is rare and valuable this close to Old City.

The layout. Two bedrooms share a level, with a separate primary suite, which makes it an easy fit for families.

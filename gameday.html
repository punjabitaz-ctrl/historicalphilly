Before and after the game

Somewhere to gather

A whole house gives a big group a kitchen, a living room and enough beds to stay together, with the restaurants, bars and history of Society Hill and Queen Village right outside.

Rideshare or taxi

The simplest door-to-door option, and the one most groups pick on game day. Car times below are typical, without game-day traffic, which is much heavier.

SEPTA subway

SEPTA's Broad Street Line ends at NRG Station, about a five-minute walk from the stadiums, and game-day service is often increased. Ask us for the best way to reach the line from your house.

Driving

Hancock House includes free parking at the house. Parking at the stadiums is separate and set by the venues, so check their official parking information before you go.

Which house

Bring everyone

Hancock House sleeps 10 and includes free parking, and Catherine House sleeps 8 right next door. Take both for up to 18, depending on availability.

Rodman House in Society Hill sleeps 8, a block from South Street and a short walk from Old City.

Stairs & accessibility

Please note: Every one of our houses is a multi-level historic home. Bedrooms and bathrooms are reached by steep, narrow stairs, and there is no elevator or step-free route. If anyone in your party has limited mobility or small children, ask us before you book.

Stairs & accessibility

Please note: Catherine House is a multi-level historic home. Bedrooms and bathrooms are reached by steep, narrow stairs, and there is no elevator or step-free route. If anyone in your party has limited mobility or small children, ask us before you book.

The house

About Catherine

Catherine House is a Trinity-style home on a historic cobblestone street in Queen Village, close to Society Hill, Head House Square, South Street and the Italian Market.

Newly remodelled with the brick and vaulted ceilings left visible, it has a mini-split heat and air unit in every room, a dedicated workspace, and a private fenced patio.

The layout. A bedroom and a bathroom on every floor, so friends can stay close together and still have space of their own.

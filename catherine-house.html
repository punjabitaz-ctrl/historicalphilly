About Catherine

Catherine House is a Trinity-style home on a historic cobblestone street in Queen Village, close to Society Hill, Head House Square, South Street and the Italian Market.

Newly remodelled with the brick and vaulted ceilings left visible, it has a mini-split heat and air unit in every room, a dedicated workspace, and a private fenced patio.

Good to know. A Trinity house means steep stairs, which can be hard for young children or anyone with mobility concerns. It is a quiet residential neighborhood, so please be respectful of neighbors.
